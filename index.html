What Petal is

Petal is a cycle and pregnancy tracker for iPhone. It shows your cycle day, your phase and your fertile window, and switches to pregnancy mode with one tap when you are ready. It includes a week by week pregnancy view and a contraction timer for the day labour starts. There is a home screen widget and an Apple Watch app.

Everything you enter stays on your phone. There is no account, no sign up, no tracking and no ads.

Petal Premium

Petal Premium unlocks the full app. Your first month is free, then it is $29.99 per year. It renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the period ends, and you can cancel any time in your Apple Account settings.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone only. Petal has no server and no account, so nothing you log is uploaded anywhere.

How do I move to a new phone?

Use the export option in Petal to save a copy of your data, then import it on the new phone.

How accurate are the predictions?

Period, fertile window and ovulation dates are calendar estimates based on the cycles you have logged. They are not a measurement and they are not a method of contraception.

What does the contraction timer do?

It times how long each contraction lasts and how far apart they are, and tells you when the pattern matches the widely published five one one guideline. It never tells you to go to hospital. It always points you back to your own midwife or maternity unit.

How do I delete everything?

Use the reset option in the app, or delete the app from your phone. Either one removes the data permanently, and there is no copy anywhere else.

Petal is for information and personal record keeping. It is not a medical device, not a method of contraception, and it does not replace advice from your midwife or doctor. If something does not feel right, contact your maternity care provider.
