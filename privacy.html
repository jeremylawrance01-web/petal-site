Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 September 2026. Contact: ellebonser14@gmail.com

Petal records some of the most personal information there is: your cycle, your symptoms and your pregnancy. It is built so that none of it ever reaches us. This policy explains exactly what that means.

What we collect

Nothing.

Every period, symptom, mood, note, due date and contraction you record is stored locally on your device. Petal has no account, no sign up, no server and no login. It contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash reporting service. Your health information is never transmitted, sold, shared or used for advertising, because it never leaves your phone in the first place.

Voice notes

The contraction timer lets you add a note by speaking instead of typing. If you use it, Petal asks for microphone and speech recognition permission and passes the audio to Apple's speech recognition service to turn it into text. Depending on your device and settings, Apple may process some speech recognition on its servers under Apple's privacy policy. The resulting text is stored only on your phone, and we never receive the audio or the text. If you never use voice notes, no microphone access is requested.

Subscriptions

Petal Premium is sold through Apple. Your purchase is processed by the App Store and we never see your payment details. Petal checks your subscription status with Apple on your device. No health information is involved in that check.

What this means

  • We cannot see your cycle, your symptoms, your pregnancy or your notes.
  • There is nothing about your health for us to hand to anyone, including advertisers, insurers, employers or authorities, and there is no server side copy to be breached or subpoenaed.
  • No third party receives your data from us, because we do not hold it.

Your control

You can export a copy of your data at any time to keep it, share it with your GP or midwife, or move it to a new phone. You can erase everything from within the app, or by deleting the app from your device. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Children

Petal is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app collects no data at all, none is collected from any user regardless of age.

Not medical advice

Petal is for information and personal record keeping. It is not a medical device and it is not a method of contraception. Cycle, fertile window and ovulation dates are calendar estimates. The contraction timer reports a widely published labour pattern guideline and always directs you to your own midwife or maternity unit. It does not diagnose, treat or make decisions for you.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Petal ever collects or transmits data, this policy will be updated before that version is released and the change will be described in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or about anything else in the app, can go to ellebonser14@gmail.com.
